About Tenancy Takeover

The Tenancy Takeover App is a simple and accessible platform designed to support students and individuals who are experiencing accommodation challenges. It helps connect people seeking housing with property listers offering tenancy takeovers by presenting clear, reliable information about available accommodations. The app aims to reduce housing stress, improve access to short-term and affordable housing, and make the accommodation search process more straightforward for users.
